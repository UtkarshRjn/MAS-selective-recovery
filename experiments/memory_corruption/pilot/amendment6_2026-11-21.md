## 2. The one question and the one figure

**Question:** when an agent's memory is damaged before an irreversible action, does it ask first, or act silently and then report success?

**Headline figure:** one figure of silent-harm rate per damage type, with Wilson confidence intervals, per model. C0 is the floor. U1 is the ceiling. Add the masking rate among harmful runs.

**Headline sentence (to complete after the data):** when the damage can be checked (F+T), and when the prompt states the profile and `.protect` are authoritative, silent harm stays under X% (the real Wilson upper bound, also reported per type). When the wrong value exists only in memory (U1), the agent acts on it, and the writer reports success in Y% of those harmful runs.
